WSU tools

Don't click it here; drag it to your bookmarks bar. Then click the bookmark on your Slate tab with a field open.

Using it

  1. In Slate, open a field so the Edit Field dialog and its editor are showing.
  2. Click the WSU tools bookmark. Its buttons appear at the right end of the editor's toolbar.
  3. After a page reload, click the bookmark again.

To make the editor taller or shorter, drag the handle at its bottom-right corner (or Tab to it and use the up and down arrows). The next field opens at that height.

  • Clean: Cleans the field with the cleaner and lists what changed.
  • Format: The official WSU formats for where the cursor is.
  • Pop-up: Adds a “(More Info)” link with a Slate pop-up.
  • WSU look: Shows the field with the WSU design system CSS while editing.

Every change is one undo step (Cmd/Ctrl+Z). Nothing is saved until you click Slate's own Save.
